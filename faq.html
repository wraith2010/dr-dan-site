Pilot FAQ

Common questions about your FAA medical exam

What is MedXPress?

MedXPress is the FAA's online application for medical certificates. You complete it before your exam, then bring the confirmation number with you so your examiner can pull up your application.

What is a special issuance?

If you have a medical condition that would normally be disqualifying, the FAA may still issue a certificate through a special issuance after reviewing your medical information. Dr. Mitchell can explain what documentation may be needed.

What happens if my application is deferred?

An examiner may defer a decision to the FAA when additional review is needed. The FAA then evaluates your case and may request more information. Dr. Mitchell can help you understand the next steps.

What should I bring?

Your MedXPress confirmation number, a photo ID, your glasses or contacts, and any relevant medical records.

Do you offer BasicMed exams?

Yes. Dr. Mitchell offers BasicMed exams in addition to first-, second- and third-class FAA medicals.
