Exams & Services

FAA medical certificates and BasicMed exams in Doylestown, PA

Whether you fly for an airline, for work, or for fun, Dr. Mitchell offers first-, second- and third-class FAA medical exams, along with BasicMed exams.

First-class medical

Required for airline transport pilots exercising the privileges of an ATP certificate.

Second-class medical

For commercial pilots and others flying for compensation or hire.

Third-class medical

For student, recreational and private pilots.

BasicMed exam

Comprehensive medical examination for pilots flying under BasicMed.

Before your exam

Complete your FAA MedXPress application online and bring your confirmation number, a photo ID, your glasses or contacts, and any relevant medical records.

What to bring

  • MedXPress confirmation number
  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Glasses or contact lenses, if you use them
  • Relevant medical records, if applicable
